Stay informed without the clutter — news tailored to you, simple and real.

🎯 Our Mission

To make news consumption simple, smart, and personalized by bringing you real-time updates across India, World, Technology, Sports, Business, Health, Science, and Entertainment.

🚀 Our Vision

To empower every reader with transparent, fact-checked, and bias-aware news — ensuring you stay informed with confidence and clarity.

⚙️ How It Works

🧠

1. Analyze Interests

Our AI learns from your reading preferences and suggests relevant articles automatically.

🌐

2. Fetch Trusted Sources

We collect headlines from verified publishers and APIs like NewsAPI, The Guardian, and BBC.

🧾

3. Filter & Rank

Articles are sorted by credibility, freshness, and relevance — ensuring quality over quantity.

💡

4. AI Insights

Each story comes with bias detection and sentiment analysis so you can see the whole picture.

🧩 Technology Stack

  • Frontend: Angular, TypeScript, HTML, CSS
  • Backend: Spring Boot (Java), REST APIs
  • Database: MySql / MongoDB (for user preferences & feeds)
  • AI/ML: Python (NLP, sentiment & bias detection models)
